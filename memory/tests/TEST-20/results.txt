WORSE THAN NOT COMPRESSING on 6 of 8 columns. Chimp/Chimp128/Elf/Patas are
refinements of the same residual and inherit this. Dead.

ALP exponent scope is the whole trick:
  column        text+xz   column-wide e   per-1024-vector e
  latitude       51,505          70,815              43,785
  longitude      53,801          72,982              44,729
  dmin           34,505          48,369              27,766
  depthError     33,103          46,389              26,446
  rms            17,599          16,766              13,318
Column-wide = 37% WORSE than text+xz. Per-vector = 8-24% BETTER.
This is the mechanism behind the reverted ragged-decimal result.

THE DECIMAL COUNT IS FREE. On this corpus the printed cell IS the shortest
round-trip decimal of its double (Python repr, same class as Ryu):
  latitude 16,190/16,190 | longitude 16,190/16,190 | depth 16,190/16,190
  mag 16,190/16,190 | rms 16,190/16,190 | depthError 16,190/16,190
  dmin 16,121/16,190 (rest blank) | magError 15,982/16,190 (rest blank)
ZERO cells need trailing zeros. So exact-double IMPLIES exact-text and the
format-descriptor stream that sank the earlier attempt need not exist.
(The explicit (significand, dec-count) split was measured anyway: at most
+5.5%, negative on 6 of 11 columns, -32% on time, -130% on updated.)

================================================================ G. lit
G. LITERATURE, the part that is a controlled measurement

Petri & Moffat, SPE 48(4) 2018 -- holds data and general compressor fixed,
varies only the pre-transform. Bits/posting, Gov2 URL docid gaps:
                 raw u32   VByte   Simple-16
  none             32.00    8.79        4.68
  xz -6             3.22    2.98        3.36
Simple-16 is the best standalone code and the WORST input to xz -- worse than
feeding xz raw uint32. Mechanism: variable-width packing shifts identical
source values to arbitrary bit phases and a byte-oriented matcher stops
matching them.  doi 10.1002/spe.2556

DuckDB #18984: Parquet V2 DELTA_BINARY_PACKED + zstd is 2.86x LARGER than
PLAIN + zstd on 5.46 M rows. Open, no fix.

Bitshuffle (Masui 2015): bit-PLANE TRANSPOSITION + LZ4 = 27.9% vs byte-shuffle
+ LZ4 47.3%. The distinction that survives is alignment-PRESERVING (transpose,
BYTE_STREAM_SPLIT) vs alignment-DESTROYING (variable-width packing).

Simple8b / PFOR / NewPFD / OptPFD / FastPFOR / SIMD-BP128 / StreamVByte /
DELTA_BINARY_PACKED: all speed codecs, all benchmarked against varint, Snappy,
zstd-default or uncompressed tuples. SIMD-BP128 is 40% worse than Simple-8b
purely from vectorising. TurboPFor is GPL-2.0; FastPFor's README states its
decoder does not validate input (invariant 3 violation in the act of adopting).

FCBench (VLDB 2024) benchmarks 13 FP codecs and its own scope note EXCLUDES
xz/LZMA, bzip2, brotli as baselines. Essentially nobody in the time-series
compression literature benchmarks against xz. That absence is the finding.

liblzma pb defaults to 2 (4-byte alignment). For byte-aligned numeric streams
pb=0 is a filter-dict change with no format implications. Untested.
