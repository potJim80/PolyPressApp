Matrix-shaped results, 2026-07-29 at commit 6d08229.

Three tables fetched by benchmarks/fetch_matrix.py -- the daily US Treasury
par yield curve 1990-2025, ten hourly sensor channels over ten years, and
hourly temperature at 24 European cities. No credentials, one command.

These are the shape the planar (2D) predictor exists for, and until this file
existed that predictor's claim rested on data no benchmark in the repository
touched.

  weather_hourly   87,672 x 11    503,460 vs 1,078,640 xz -9e    2.14x
  treasury_yields   9,006 x 9      34,891 vs    64,836 xz -9e    1.86x

Fetching this data is also what exposed the all-or-nothing numeric gate: the
yield curve contains four blank cells in 72,048 and they were dropping all
eight rate columns to the dictionary path, costing 41.2% of the archive. See
matrix-findings-2026-07-29.txt.
