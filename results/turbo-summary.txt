WHAT TURBO CHANGES
------------------

  * independent binary streams, so every decision is measured on the
    column itself instead of by encoding the whole table twice
  * the string groups stay in ONE pile -- splitting them costs up to 13.2%
  * numeric cross-column prediction (a column as its difference from
    another column) -- 26.2% of the packed numeric payload on nested
    aggregates
  * derived string columns (a column as a template over other columns) --
    traffic_incidents -32.7%, its `point` and `id` become formulas
  * threads: lzma and bz2 release the GIL

WHAT IT GIVES UP

  * no byte-identity with the C encoder; there is no C port
  * no 2D planar predictor
  * the row-wise candidate is chosen by a sample nominator plus a measured
    race, not by always encoding both ways
