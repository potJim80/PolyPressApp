======================================================

branch `turbo`, polypress/turbo.py, container PPZT.
Reproduce: python3 benchmarks/bench_turbo.py <dir> --enc fast turbo
Every archive is decoded and compared cell for cell before its size
is allowed to count.

WHAT TURBO CHANGES
------------------

  * independent binary streams, so every decision is measured on the
    column itself instead of by encoding the whole table twice
  * the string groups stay in ONE pile -- splitting them costs up to 13.2%
  * numeric cross-column prediction (a column as its difference from
    another column) -- 26.2% of the packed numeric payload on nested
    aggregates
  * derived string columns (a column as a template over other columns) --
    traffic_incidents -32.7%, its `point` and `id` become formulas
  * threads: lzma and bz2 release the GIL

WHAT IT GIVES UP

  * no byte-identity with the C encoder; there is no C port
  * no 2D planar predictor
  * the row-wise candidate is chosen by a sample nominator plus a measured
    race, not by always encoding both ways

SUPERSEDED BY THE 500-DATASET SWEEP -- see socrata500-polypress-turbo-summary.txt
================================================================================
The numbers above are from an 8-dataset bench set and a 63-dataset subset, and
they UNDERSTATE the fork: the speedup grows with file size (2.07x under 12 MB,
3.97x at 25 MB, 6.18x at 50 MB). The real record is the 500 sweep.

    480 unselected datasets, 3.26 GB of CSV, 13.9M rows, 480/480 exact
    turbo wins 440 of 480 (92%) against the best of 17 competitors
    median margin 1.25x, best 2.73x, worst 0.65x, aggregate 1.27x
