Sizes are FREE mode (permutation not stored) to isolate the value of the
ordering from the 3-4% cost of remembering it, measured separately.

CAVEAT on the already-grouped ratio: it is only informative for LOW
cardinality columns. For a near-unique column breaks ~= n-1 and ideal
~= n-1, so the ratio is 1.0 automatically and means nothing.
