5. Speed, indicative only
-------------------------
One timing pass per codec, taken while the sweep had the machine
to itself but with no attempt to quiet it. Sizes are exact and
reproducible; these numbers are not. Read them for order of
magnitude and nothing finer.

And they are NOT measured on the same basis, which matters more
than the noise does. The general-purpose tools are handed the raw
CSV bytes, because that is what compressing a CSV actually is.
Parquet, ORC, Feather and Polypress are handed an already-parsed
table, so none of them is charged for reading the CSV -- the cost
every one of them would really pay. Read the columnar rows and the
polypress row against each other; read either against gzip only as
